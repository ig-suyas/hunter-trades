NOTES

-   These categories contain different types of technical-analysis
    concepts; they are not all traditional chart patterns.
-   Candlestick patterns describe one or a small group of candles.
    Harmonic patterns use Fibonacci-based price relationships. Wyckoff
    structures describe phases or events in market behavior.
-   A pattern does not guarantee a reversal or continuation. Context,
    volume, timeframe, market structure, and confirmation all matter.
